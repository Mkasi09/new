## Full description

PHEPHA MV ISDP is a secure field-service workflow app for authorised PHEPHA MV
teams. It connects administrators, supervisors, and technicians throughout the
complete work-order lifecycle.

Administrators can create jobs, monitor operational progress, review submitted
work, manage approvals, and prepare completed work for billing. Supervisors can
accept work, coordinate teams, and assign technicians. Technicians can review
job details, confirm arrival by scanning the site QR code, capture before-and-
after evidence, record completion details, collect customer sign-off, and submit
work for review.

Key capabilities include:

- Role-based dashboards for administrators, supervisors, and technicians
- Work-order creation, assignment, status tracking, and audit history
- QR-based site-arrival confirmation
- Before-and-after photo evidence
- Technician notes, issue reporting, and customer sign-off
- Job chat and in-app support messaging
- Review, approval, decline, and resubmission workflows
- Operational notifications
- Limited offline work-order access with automatic synchronisation
- Invoice and billing-readiness support

PHEPHA MV ISDP is intended only for authorised organisational users. An account
provided by an authorised administrator is required.
