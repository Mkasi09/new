# PHEPHA MV ISDP Support Details

## Public store contact

- **Support email:** info@softwap.co.za
- **Developer/organisation:** Softwap (Pty) Ltd
- **App name:** PHEPHA MV ISDP
- **Package name:** za.co.phephamv.isdp
- **Privacy-policy file:** `release/play-store/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`

Before submission, publish the privacy policy on a public HTTPS page and place
that URL in the Play Console. The support email should be monitored by a team
able to verify users and escalate operational or privacy requests.

## Information users should include

- Their work email address and role
- Job number or site code, when relevant
- Device manufacturer/model and Android version
- App version (`1.0.0`, build `1` for this release)
- A concise description of the issue and when it occurred
- A screenshot with customer names, signatures, addresses, credentials, and
  other confidential information removed unless support specifically requires it

Users must never send passwords, temporary passwords, private keys, or one-time
codes to support.

## Suggested support categories

- Sign-in or password reset
- Missing or incorrect role/access
- Work order or assignment issue
- QR scan or camera issue
- Evidence upload or customer sign-off issue
- Notification issue
- Invoice or approval issue
- Privacy access, correction, or deletion request
- Suspected security incident

## Internal handling notes

- Verify the requester before changing account access or disclosing job data.
- Treat evidence photos, customer signatures, site addresses, and chat messages
  as confidential.
- Record and escalate suspected account compromise immediately.
- Route privacy requests to the authorised privacy/management contact and retain
  a record of the response.
- Do not request a user's password.
