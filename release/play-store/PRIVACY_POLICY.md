# PHEPHA MV ISDP Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 July 2026  
**Technical support and privacy contact:** Softwap (Pty) Ltd, info@softwap.co.za

PHEPHA MV provides PHEPHA MV ISDP, an internal field-service application used
to create, assign, execute, review, and approve work orders. Softwap (Pty) Ltd
develops and provides technical support for the application. This policy
explains how information is handled when authorised employees, contractors,
and service personnel use the application.

## Information we collect

Depending on your role and how you use the app, we process:

- **Account and profile information:** name, work email address, user ID, role,
  team, authentication status, and password-change status. Passwords are
  processed by Firebase Authentication and are not displayed to PHEPHA MV.
- **Work-order information:** job number, site name and address, site code,
  scope, priority, deadlines, assignment, workflow status, timestamps, notes,
  issue reports, parts or materials, and approval history.
- **Evidence and customer sign-off:** photos selected or captured for a job,
  evidence status, customer name, and customer signature.
- **Communications:** job-chat messages, support requests, sender details,
  timestamps, and read status.
- **Device and notification information:** Firebase or Huawei push-notification
  token, notification permission/status, and token update time.
- **Local app data:** a limited offline cache of accessible work orders,
  notification-read state, and app preferences stored on the device.

The app requests camera access only for QR scanning and capturing job evidence.
It requests notification permission to provide work-order alerts. It does not
request device location or contacts, and it does not use advertising IDs.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- authenticate users and enforce administrator, supervisor, and technician
  permissions;
- create, assign, complete, review, approve, decline, and audit work orders;
- verify site arrival using a QR code;
- retain job evidence and customer sign-off;
- support job-related and technical-support communication;
- deliver operational notifications;
- provide limited offline access and synchronise changes when connectivity
  returns;
- secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service; and
- comply with contractual, operational, audit, and legal obligations.

## Legal basis

We process information where necessary to provide and administer the
field-service platform, fulfil employment or service-contract obligations,
protect legitimate operational and security interests, and comply with
applicable law. Where consent is legally required, it will be obtained before
the relevant processing.

## Storage and service providers

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Authentication, Cloud
Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. It may
also use Huawei Push Kit on compatible Huawei devices. These providers process
information on our behalf to operate the app. Information may be processed in
countries outside South Africa, subject to appropriate contractual and legal
safeguards.

We do not sell personal information and do not use app data for advertising.
We do not share personal information with independent third parties except when
required by law, necessary to protect users or the service, or authorised by
the organisation responsible for the relevant work order.

## Security

Information is encrypted in transit using secure network connections. Access
to backend information is restricted by authenticated role and work-order
assignment. We also use access rules, audit fields, and administrative controls.
No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so users must protect
their credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account, work-order, evidence, communication, and audit information
for as long as needed for active operations, contractual records, legal or
audit requirements, dispute handling, and security. Local cached work-order
data can be cleared from the app and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

To request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal
information, email **info@softwap.co.za**. Requests may require
identity and authority verification. Some information may be retained where
required by law, contract, security, or legitimate audit needs. Because user
accounts are provisioned by authorised administrators, account closure requests
are also handled through the organisation's administrator or the support email.

## Children's privacy

PHEPHA MV ISDP is a business application for authorised workers and is not
directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, service providers, or legal requirements
change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is
published.
